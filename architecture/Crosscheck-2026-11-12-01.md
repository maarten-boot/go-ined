| C1 | M | **When the Rechecker switches to a better prober, it closes the old one at once.** <br>• A ping round that started on the old prober then fails, with "prober is closed" or a socket error in raw mode, and the monitor counts it as a failed round. <br>• With the default `down_after = 2` that is one false miss; with `down_after = 1` the host turns `down`. <br>• It happens when ICMP improves while the server runs, for example IPv6 appearing next to IPv4 | Probe 2: `Rechecker.current` closes `old` right after the swap; `ICMPProber.rawRound` returns `errClosed`; `monitor.finish` treats any error other than `ErrFamilyUnavailable` as a failed round | Close the old prober only after a grace period (2 minutes, longer than any round), in the background. Test with a fake clock: not closed at once, closed later |
| C2 | L | **A right-click on one of several selected items replaces the selection with that item.** The context menu then acts on that item only, and the multi-selection is gone.
