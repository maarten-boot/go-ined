## 2. Code → Spec (bugs found in the code)

| # | Sev | Finding | Evidence | Proposed fix |
|---|-----|---------|----------|--------------|
| C1 | L | **The menu counts items differently from the keys when a group and something inside it are both selected.** <br>• Example: select group G and host h in G, then right-click h. The menu says "Move 2 items to group…" and "Delete 2 items…". <br>• But only G is acted on, since h goes with its group. <br>• The Delete key opens G's normal delete dialog, with "move contents up" or "delete everything". The menu opens "Delete 1 group? … the contents move up", which has no cascade choice | Probe 1: `menuItems` counts `targets` before `topLevel`, and always calls `deleteManyDialog` for more than one target; `deleteSelected` filters with `topLevel` first and uses `deleteDialog` for one | Count and act on `topLevel(selection)` in the menu, as the keys do: with one top-level item, show the usual labels for it and use `deleteDialog`. | C2 | L | **"Longer than any ping round" isn't guaranteed.** <br>• The config has no upper limit on `count` or `timeout`, so `count = 30`, `timeout = "5s"` makes a round of about 150 s, longer than `retireAfter`. <br>• A prober replaced during such a round is still closed under it. <br>• With the defaults (3 × 1 s) a round is about 3 s, so this needs an unusual config. <br>• Also, `Rechecker.Close` doesn't close a prober that is waiting to be retired (only at process exit) | Probe 2: `config.go` checks only `count ≥ 1` and `timeout > 0`; `recheck.go` `retireAfter = 2 * time.Minute` | Count the rounds in flight per prober, and close a replaced prober when its last round ends (at once if none runs). That replaces the timer and doesn't depend on any duration. `Close` also closes retired probers. SPEC-304: "closed when the rounds using it have finished". Test with a blocking fake prober |

## 9. Resolution (user: "OK, all")

Applied on `fix/crosscheck-11`:
- **C1:** `menuItems` uses `topLevel(selection)`. With more than one top-level item: "Move N items…" and "Delete N items…" (N counted after filtering). With only a group left: `Move group "lan" to group…` and `Delete group "lan"…`, which open the group's own dialogs, so "delete everything" is offered again. TestMultiSelectMenuGroupAndMember (TC-727). SPEC-724 v0.31.
- **C2:** the Rechecker counts the rounds per prober. A replaced prober is closed when its last round ends, or at once if none runs. There is no timer any more. `Close` also closes replaced probers that are still in use, each only once, and nothing is opened after it. TestRechecker (a round blocked across the switch) and TestRecheckerRetire (TC-315). SPEC-304 v0.31.

`make all`, `make lint` (staticcheck, govulncheck) pass. `Changes.md` `[Unreleased]` has both under "Fixed".
