## 2. Code → Spec (bugs found in the code)

| # | Sev | Finding | Proposed fix |
|---|-----|---------|--------------|
| C1 | M | **"Reflow…" on a collapsed group undersizes its expanded subgroups.** `ReflowCell` takes each member's size from the layout. Members of a collapsed group aren't in the layout, so it estimates them as icons, even when a member is an expanded group whose frame will show once the parent expands. Probe: with A collapsed the cell is **80 × 92**; with A expanded it is **480 × 428**. After a Reflow on the collapsed A, expanding A shows B's frame over the host next to it. SPEC-723 says the cell fits "the biggest member … or its whole frame". The menu offers Reflow on collapsed groups too. The positions are shared, but a second Reflow after expanding repairs them. Present since Reflow (v0.28); not caused by the crosscheck-15 fix. | In `ReflowCell`, lay out with the reflowed group and its ancestors treated as expanded (restored afterwards, as `WrapNames` already is), so hidden expanded subgroups are measured as frames. Test (TC-724): the probe above, with the same cell collapsed and expanded. |

Checked and fine:
- **The frame rule:** the widened frame grows only to the right, so the corner rules of SPEC-703 (collapse and expand keep the top-left corner) are unchanged, and `FrameIfExpanded` gets the same width from the layout. A parent frame includes a widened child frame, because it uses the child's final rect.
- **Bold measuring** is used consistently: name wrapping, label boxes, the Reflow cell and the drawing now all measure what is drawn bold. Status words, drawn regular, get a few pixels to spare, as the comment says.
- **Zoom:** text is drawn at `mapTextSize × zoom`; the 14-pixel padding on the right covers the small non-linearity of font widths.
